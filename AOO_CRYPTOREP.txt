ARTICLES OF ORGANIZATION
OF
CRYPTO REP, A NONPROFIT PUBLIC BENEFIT CORPORATION

ARTICLE I - NAME
The name of this corporation is Crypto Rep.

ARTICLE II - PURPOSE
This corporation is a nonprofit public benefit corporation and is not organized for the private gain of any person.
It is organized under the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law for charitable and educational purposes.
Specific purpose: To build and maintain open-source infrastructure that tracks, attributes, and pays royalties
to software developers based on code usage, thereby promoting sustainable open-source development.
